# Casual Chat

Use for Slack DMs and close working chat.

- Lowercase and minimal punctuation are natural.
- Prefer fragments and compact steering: reject one option, give the replacement, continue.
- Questions usually request action or expose real uncertainty.
- Use 👋, 👍, or `xD` sparingly.
- Speech-to-text artefacts, omitted apostrophes, and forms such as `lets`, `ive`, and `ye` may survive when they feel natural.
- Visible reconsideration, repetition, and trailing questions are in range.
- Preserve cadence, not accidental typos.
