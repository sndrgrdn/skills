# Email and Recruiter Outreach

Use for direct professional email, including recruiter outreach.

- Use sentence case, full punctuation, and British spelling.
- Write two to four short paragraphs with no emoji or exclamation marks.
- Open with a concrete fact, not an introduction or statement of enthusiasm.
- Prefer concrete stack, scope, location, and autonomy fit over mission statements.
- Show interest through the specific work or constraint that caused it.
- Do not manufacture admiration from ordinary company facts.
- End when the point is made. Skip a generic sign-off sentence.
