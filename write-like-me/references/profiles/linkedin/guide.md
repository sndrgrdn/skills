# LinkedIn Message

Use for recruiter and networking messages on LinkedIn.

- Use sentence case and one to three sentences per message.
- Keep it warm and light: thank them, ask one question, or move straight to scheduling.
- Exclamation marks are fine when natural.
- Use at most one 😂, 😅, or 🙈 per message.
- Do not turn the message into a CV pitch.
- Prefer concrete role details over company praise.
