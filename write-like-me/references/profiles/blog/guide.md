# Blog Post and Article

Use for personal-site prose, blog posts, and articles.

- Use sentence case and full punctuation. Length follows the subject.
- Fragments can control rhythm. Longer connected paragraphs should still sound spoken rather than composed by committee.
- Dry humour and honest swearing are in range when earned by the point.
- Personal examples and concrete work carry the argument.
- Avoid manifesto energy, second-person lecturing, and a concluding lesson that merely restates the piece.
- End when the final observation lands.
