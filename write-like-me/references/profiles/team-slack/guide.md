# Team Slack

Use for messages and announcements to a team channel.

- Keep it tight, with slightly more connective tissue than a direct message.
- Case and punctuation depend on the relationship and seriousness.
- Prefer a plain `hey guys` or the point itself over affected slang such as `yo`.
- One sparing emoji can carry the warmth.
- Corrections and technical requests use the same compact steering as casual chat.
- Clean transcription errors that could distract or create ambiguity.
