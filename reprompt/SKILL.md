---
name: reprompt
description: Rework a prompt through altitude, depth, and range into a stronger task brief.
disable-model-invocation: true
---

Rewrite the supplied prompt or current conversational task to enlist capabilities its framing leaves unused.

Treat the request as a hypothesis about the work needed, not a ceiling on what can be accomplished. Challenge it through three lenses:

- **Altitude: question the task.** What should happen next? Work backward from that outcome. Consider whether a different task or durable workflow would serve it better, and what access, environment, and authority would enable the recipient to own it. Test a proposed replacement against history or alongside the existing process. When the artifact itself is the creative goal, keep it.
- **Depth: raise the standard.** What would a field-leading practitioner do that the request leaves out? Start from their rawest evidence and actual instruments. Consider a step-change target, try to falsify accepted assumptions, and seek discoveries beyond reproducing known answers. Validate discoveries on evidence not used to develop them. Choose an outside judge that can reject the result, preferably an executable feedback loop.
- **Range: cross disciplinary boundaries.** Express the work as actions rather than artifact nouns. Imagine a crew from disciplines that could improve those actions; turn their contributions into concrete methods and tools applied to this task.

Keep a change only if it enables a substantively better approach, target, or test. When none does, leave the request largely intact. Preserve explicit constraints and separate inferred intent from requirements. Present changed scope and ambitious targets as proposals.

Settle goal-changing unknowns with the user; turn other missing inputs into requirements to obtain. Inspect available sources only as needed to ground the rewrite.

Return one concise, self-contained prompt.
