# Pattern 36: Phrasal templates and unfilled placeholders

Open this when the text may contain **phrasal templates and unfilled placeholders**.

## Signals

[Your Name]; [Describe section]; INSERT_SOURCE_URL; PASTE_URL_HERE; 2025-XX-XX

## Why it reads AI-generated

The model generated a fill-in template and the placeholders were not replaced.

## False positives

- Human writers may use this pattern deliberately for genre, emphasis, or local style.
- Treat one instance as weak evidence. Treat repeated use with other tells as stronger.
- Preserve the pattern if it is required by a style guide, quote, code, citation, or the user's sample voice.

## Rewrite strategy

Fill only from known information. Otherwise mark missing data or remove the section.

## Before

> Please see [link to revised article]. Best regards, [Your Name].

## After

> Please see the revised document linked above.

## Audit question

Does the rewritten sentence say something specific that the original actually supports, or did it merely hide the tell?
