# Pattern 15: Biology and ecosystem padding

Open this when the text may contain **biology and ecosystem padding**.

## Signals

crucial for ecosystem; preservation is vital; supports biodiversity; conservation efforts are ongoing

## Why it reads AI-generated

LLMs overstate ecological or conservation importance for species with little evidence.

## False positives

- Human writers may use this pattern deliberately for genre, emphasis, or local style.
- Treat one instance as weak evidence. Treat repeated use with other tells as stronger.
- Preserve the pattern if it is required by a style guide, quote, code, citation, or the user's sample voice.

## Rewrite strategy

State known range, habitat, status, and documented threats only.

## Before

> Preserving this plant is vital for ecological diversity and cultural heritage.

## After

> The plant is endemic to western Maui. Its conservation status has not been assessed.

## Audit question

Does the rewritten sentence say something specific that the original actually supports, or did it merely hide the tell?
