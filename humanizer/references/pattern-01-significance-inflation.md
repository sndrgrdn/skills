# Pattern 01: Significance, legacy, and broader-trend inflation

Open this when the text may contain **significance, legacy, and broader-trend inflation**.

## Signals

pivotal moment; testament/reminder; underscores significance; broader/evolving landscape; enduring legacy; focal point; deeply rooted

## Why it reads AI-generated

The prose inflates ordinary facts into universal meaning. It replaces the specific thing that happened with ceremonial importance language.

## False positives

- Human writers may use this pattern deliberately for genre, emphasis, or local style.
- Treat one instance as weak evidence. Treat repeated use with other tells as stronger.
- Preserve the pattern if it is required by a style guide, quote, code, citation, or the user's sample voice.

## Rewrite strategy

Replace grandeur with concrete actors, dates, actions, limits, or observable effects. If no concrete fact supports the importance claim, cut it.

## Before

> The archive stands as a testament to the city's enduring commitment to preserving cultural memory.

## After

> The city opened the archive in 1984. It holds council minutes, planning records, and donated photographs.

## Audit question

Does the rewritten sentence say something specific that the original actually supports, or did it merely hide the tell?
