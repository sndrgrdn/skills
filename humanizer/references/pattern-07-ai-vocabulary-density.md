# Pattern 07: High-density AI vocabulary clusters

Open this when the text may contain **high-density ai vocabulary clusters**.

## Signals

additionally; align with; bolstered; crucial; delve; enhance; foster; intricate; key; landscape; meticulous; pivotal; robust; showcase; tapestry; underscore; valuable; vibrant

## Why it reads AI-generated

One word may be normal. A cluster makes the prose sound model-generated, especially when the words do no real work.

## False positives

- Human writers may use this pattern deliberately for genre, emphasis, or local style.
- Treat one instance as weak evidence. Treat repeated use with other tells as stronger.
- Preserve the pattern if it is required by a style guide, quote, code, citation, or the user's sample voice.

## Rewrite strategy

Replace clustered abstractions with simpler words or concrete nouns. Keep technical/domain terms when they are precise.

## Before

> Additionally, the initiative underscores a pivotal shift in the education landscape.

## After

> The initiative changed how the district assigns teachers to first-year classes.

## Audit question

Does the rewritten sentence say something specific that the original actually supports, or did it merely hide the tell?
